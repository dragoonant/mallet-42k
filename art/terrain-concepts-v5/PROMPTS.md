# Terrain concepts v5 (Gemini, 2026-10-16)

Painted grimdark terrain, one piece per image, white background. Each image went through the Hunyuan3D
terrain chain on Alteisen (cutout -> 2.1 shape -> 2.0 paint) and became `public/assets/terrain/ruins/t5-<name>.glb`.

Every prompt = framing + subject + shared tail.

Shared tail: "A fully painted tabletop wargame terrain piece for a grimdark far-future battlefield, 28mm scale:
weathered grey rockcrete, rusted dark iron, tarnished brass trim, soot and grime. Isolated on a plain pure white
background, whole object in frame with margin, soft even studio light. No base board, no figures, no text, no logos,
no skulls."

Framing:
- walls: "one freestanding ruined wall section, a single flat wall panel with very shallow depth, seen from the front
  and slightly to one side."
- crates: "one low terrain piece about twice as long as it is wide and about as tall as it is wide, seen from a
  three-quarter view from above."
- barricades: "one long, low, narrow barricade about three times as long as it is tall, seen from a three-quarter view."
- craters: "one shallow round shell crater terrain piece, low and flat, seen from a high three-quarter view from above."
